# Glossary

Swedish shooting terms as the app uses them.

Fält
: Field shooting. Moving between stations and shooting varied targets at
unknown distances.

Föreningsintyg
: The certificate a club issues confirming your activity, used in a licence
renewal.

Guldserie
: A perfect series — every shot in the ten ring.

Klass
: Your competition class as a shooter. See
[Vapengrupp and klass](vapengrupp-and-klass.md).

Kretsfältskjutning
: A district-level field shooting competition.

Militär snabbmatch
: Military rapid fire. Series shot against a time limit.

Precision
: Precision shooting. Slow, deliberate, scored on ring value.

Provserie
: A sighting series — shot to check your sights, not counted in the score.
The app calls this a **sighter** and shows its score as N/A.

Riksmästarklass
: Klass 3, the top shooter class.

Serie
: A series: the run of shots scored together — five in precision, six in
militär snabbmatch.

Skjutlag
: Squad, or patrol. The group you shoot with, and the unit start times are
assigned to.

Standardmedalj
: A standard medal, awarded for reaching a score threshold in a competition.

Särskjutning
: A shoot-off, to separate shooters on equal scores.

Vapengrupp
: Which firearm class you are shooting. See
[Vapengrupp and klass](vapengrupp-and-klass.md).

Veteran yngre / äldre
: Veteran classes, from the year you turn 60 and 70 respectively.
