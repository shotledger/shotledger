# Glossary

Swedish shooting terms as the app uses them.

Fält
: Field shooting. Moving between stations and shooting varied targets at
unknown distances.

Figurskjutning
: Figure shooting — delmoment C of Nationell helmatch: four 5-shot series at
18, 16, 14 and 12 seconds on the figure target, no sighter. The app also
offers it on its own, as **Figure Shooting**, for training. The figure target
has four ringed figures worth 6 to 10; the app scores it on the keypad only,
because the rule book gives no dimensions for photo scoring to work from.

Föreningsintyg
: The certificate a club issues confirming your activity, used in a licence
renewal.

Guldserie
: A perfect series — every shot in the ten ring.

Gren (event)
: An ISSF competition item — a discipline shot in one category, such as
10 m Air Pistol Women. ISSF puts the category on the event; SPSF puts it on
the class. See [Weapon group and class](weapon-group-and-class.md).

Grovpistol
: Centre fire pistol — ISSF's 25 m Centre Fire Pistol, the sport pistol
course shot with a 7.62–9.65 mm gun. SvSF runs it at SM as one open class.

Helmatch
: Nationell helmatch — the app's **National Combined Match**. Three 25 m parts
of four 5-shot series each, 200 points per part and 600 in all: A precision,
B rapid fire on the rapid-fire target, and C figurskjutning. **Full Match**
lays it out with a sighter before A and before B, and none before C.

Klass
: Your competition class as a shooter — the app calls it **Class**. See
[Weapon group and class](weapon-group-and-class.md).

Kretsfältskjutning
: A district-level field shooting competition.

Militär snabbmatch
: Military rapid fire. Series shot against a time limit.

Omskjutning
: A re-shoot of a station after a faulty target or a weapon malfunction. The
re-shot result is the one that counts.

Poängmål
: A ringed figure on a field shooting station. Its points separate equal
results; they are never part of the result itself.

Precision
: Precision shooting. Slow, deliberate, scored on ring value.

Provserie
: A sighting series — shot to check your sights, not counted in the score.
The app calls this a **sighter** and shows its score as N/A.

Riksmästarklass
: Class 3, the top shooter class.

Serie
: A series: the run of shots scored together — five in precision, six in
militär snabbmatch.

Skjutlag
: Squad, or patrol. The group you shoot with, and the unit start times are
assigned to.

Snabbluftpistol
: Rapid fire air pistol — SvSF's own 10 m event, shot like 25 m rapid fire
but with 10, 8 and 6 second series. Not an ISSF event.

Standardmedalj
: A standard medal, awarded for reaching a score threshold in a competition.

Särskjutning
: A shoot-off, to separate shooters on equal scores.

Station
: One firing point on a field shooting course — six shots at one figure, at
one distance, in one time.

Vapengrupp
: Which firearm class you are shooting — the app calls it **Weapon Group**. See
[Weapon group and class](weapon-group-and-class.md).

Veteran yngre / äldre
: Veteran classes, from the year you turn 60 and 70 respectively.
