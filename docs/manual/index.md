# ![ShotLedger](assets/shotledger-horizontal-light.svg#only-light){ width="360" }![ShotLedger](assets/shotledger-horizontal-dark.svg#only-dark){ width="360" }

A target-shooting logbook for pistol shooters, built around what actually
happens at the range: a series is shot, its shots are tapped in, the next
series begins.

It has no account requirement and works offline.

## What it does

- **Logs sessions shot by shot** — training and competition, under SPSF and
  ISSF programs or as free training. A fältskjutning session is logged station
  by station, with hits, figures, points and shoot-offs; a session can also
  record just the rounds fired.
- **Scores a paper target from a photograph.** You photograph the target, mark
  where the holes are, and the app works out the score from the target face's
  real geometry.
- **Tracks firearms and ammunition**, including handloads, round counts and
  maintenance.
- **Keeps the activity record** a Swedish shooter needs when renewing a firearm
  license.
- **Times your shooting** — one timer for par times, interval drills and
  IPSC-style strings, with the microphone recording your splits when you ask
  it to, plus the SPSF fälttimer with its spoken commands.
- **Shows how you are doing** — statistics filtered by period, discipline and
  tags, personal records, and a session report or share card for one
  session.
- **Imports competition results** from WebShooter, with placement and
  standardmedalj.
- **Keeps your data yours** — a backup file you can restore on a new phone,
  sessions exported as open JSON, and optional sync with a web app where you
  can read, enter and edit sessions from any browser.

## What it is not

Target shooting only. Not hunting, not tactical.

Sweden is the first market rather than the boundary: federation rules —
disciplines, programs, weapon groups (sv _vapengrupper_), shooter classes —
live in data files, and
compliance features hide themselves where no profile applies.
