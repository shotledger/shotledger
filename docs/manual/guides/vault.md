# The Vault

**Tools → Vault.** An encrypted place on your phone for the documents a
shooter carries: licence cards, club membership cards.

## How it is protected

Contents are encrypted with a key held in the phone's own secure storage, and
opening the Vault requires your device unlock — fingerprint, face or PIN.

The Vault is **excluded from backups by default**. A backup file is something
you copy to a computer, a cloud drive, an SD card; a photograph of your licence
card does not belong in a file that travels that casually.

You can include it deliberately, one backup at a time, protected by a
passphrase you choose. The app warns you before it does, and that passphrase is
not recoverable — lose it and that Vault content is gone from that backup.

## What it is not

It is not a password manager, and not a sync target. The Vault stays on the
device it was created on.
