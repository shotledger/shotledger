# License activity

Swedish firearm license applications ask your club to certify that you have
been active on the range. The app keeps that record from the sessions you are
already logging.

For what the requirement actually is, which of the two cases applies to you,
and what the app deliberately does not track, see
[How license activity is counted](../explanation/license-activity.md).

## Turning it on

**Settings → Compliance → Sweden (FAP 551-3)**. Left at **None**, none of this
appears — which is the right state outside Sweden.

Until you choose, a phone whose system language is Swedish starts on Sweden and
any other starts on None. The language you pick inside the app does not change
it.

The status then appears on Home and on every firearm: how many activities you
have logged in the window, and how many you need.

## Choosing your case

The app proposes one of the two cases from your firearms, and says why it chose
it. To overrule it:

1. **Settings → Compliance → Activity Requirement**.
2. Pick the case that applies to you.

Once you pick one it stays picked — adding, editing or deleting a firearm will
not move it again. Until then the setting follows your firearms.

If the proposed case looks wrong, read the reason line under it first: it
usually points at a missing detail on a firearm rather than at the case itself.
Firearms missing a weapon type, caliber or licence are flagged **Incomplete**
on the Firearms tab, and filling those in may move the proposal on its own.

## Seeing where you stand

Tap the status on **Home** to open **License Check**. It lists every
requirement on the club's certificate, grouped by what you are applying for:

- **New Purchase**: the activity requirement for your case, and the additional
  handgun box for each pistol or revolver marked as your own.
- **Renewal**: the renewal box for each firearm whose license has an end date.

Each one says whether it is met. If it is met, it says by when to shoot next.
If it is not, it says how many more sessions are needed, and with which firearm
where that matters. Tap a firearm's name to open it.

If an earlier year of the renewal box is short, logging sessions today cannot
fix it, and the check says so. It also gives the date from which the box can be
met if you keep logging sessions with that firearm. [Why a past year cannot be
made up](../explanation/license-activity.md#a-year-that-has-already-ended)
explains it.

## Knowing when to shoot next

When a requirement is met, the app says by which date you need to log your next
session to keep it met:

- On **Home**, under the status: "Next session by …".
- On a firearm, under each licence activity line and under each Föreningsintyg
  criterion that is met.

Log a session on or before that date and the requirement stays met for longer;
the date then moves to the next session that is about to fall out of the
period. When a requirement is not met, no date is shown. [How the date is
worked out](../explanation/license-activity.md#the-next-session-date) explains
the details.

## Checking a firearm's own criteria

Two of the form's boxes are about one gun rather than about you: applying for
an **additional handgun**, and **renewing a time-limited permit**. Those live
on the firearm, not on Home.

1. **Firearms → the firearm**.
2. Scroll to **Föreningsintyg criteria**.

Each criterion shows whether it is met, and the count behind it — one line per
year for the renewal criterion, because it is checked year by year. Only
sessions you logged **with that firearm** count, so if the numbers look low,
check that your sessions record which gun you shot.

A criterion you expected and cannot see is not being hidden: the app only shows
the additional-handgun box for a pistol or revolver marked **My Own**, and the
renewal box for a firearm whose most recent licence has an end date. [Which
firearms each one is shown
for](../explanation/license-activity.md#which-firearms-each-one-is-shown-for)
says what to fix.

## Getting it out

The app counts your sessions; it does not produce the log you hand to the
förening. A row on that log is evidence only once someone from the club has
signed it, and that happens in the club's paper logbook — so there is no PDF
of the log to print. Keep the logbook signed as you go, and use the app to
see where you stand.

**Settings → Backup & Data** exports every session as data.
